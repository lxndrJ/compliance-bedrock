# REACH — Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (Summary)

**Scope:** REACH applies to all chemical substances on their own, in mixtures, and in articles manufactured or imported into the EU in quantities of one tonne or more per year. Textile, sportswear, and consumer-goods products fall under the "articles" provisions.

**Key obligations for product launches in EU markets (e.g. Germany, France):**

1. **Registration:** Substances intentionally released from articles must be registered with ECHA if above one tonne per year.
2. **Restrictions (Annex XVII):** Products must not contain restricted substances above limit values, including:
   - Azodyes that may release carcinogenic amines (applicable to textiles)
   - Cadmium, lead, and their compounds in plastics and surface coatings
   - Phthalates (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DIBP) in plasticized materials
   - Nonylphenol and nonylphenol ethoxylates in textiles (detergent residue limits)
3. **SVHC and Article 33:** If a Candidate List substance is present above 0.1% weight-by-weight, customers must be informed and the information must be provided on request.
4. **Notification (Article 7.2):** SVHCs in articles above one tonne per year must be notified to ECHA.

**Evidence required for compliance sign-off:**
- A valid REACH conformity certificate or declaration of compliance issued by an accredited testing body (e.g. SGS, TUV, Intertek)
- Test reports for restricted substances covering the product's materials
- The certificate must be valid (not expired) at the planned launch date

**Reject rule:** A product without a valid REACH certificate must not be launched in any EU market. Missing or expired certificates require renewal before launch.
