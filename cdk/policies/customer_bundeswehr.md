# Customer Policy — Bundeswehr

**Customer:** Bundeswehr (German Armed Forces) — BAAINBw procurement framework
**Category:** Tactical textiles, footwear, and equipment

## Mandatory supplier requirements

1. **ISO 9001 certification:** Suppliers must hold a valid ISO 9001 quality management certificate. The certificate must cover the production site used for the product.
2. **Supplier audit:** Suppliers must pass a technical and quality audit performed by or on behalf of BAAINBw. Audit results are valid for 36 months.
3. **NATO quality assurance terms:** Deliveries may require AQAP-2111 quality assurance terms in the contract; verify per contract before launch.

## Documentation requirements

- All certificates must be provided in German or with certified German translation.
- Traceability of materials must be documented down to the batch level.

## Reject rule

A product from a supplier without a valid ISO 9001 certificate or without a passed Bundeswehr audit must be rejected for launch. Missing documents require human review by the compliance officer; the agent must not approve based on pending audits.
