# Customer Policy — Decathlon

**Customer:** Decathlon S.A.
**Category:** Sporting goods, textiles, and hardgoods

## Mandatory supplier requirements

1. **Supplier audit:** Every supplier must have passed a social/environmental audit (e.g. BSCI, SMETA, or Decathlon's own assessment) within the last 24 months. Products from suppliers without a passed audit must be rejected.
2. **OEKO-TEX Standard 100:** All textile products in direct contact with skin must carry a valid OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificate. The certificate must name the product or the article group and must not be expired.

## Documentation requirements

- Certificates must be issued by accredited bodies and uploaded to the supplier portal before first order.
- Decathlon requires the supplier to notify any change in production site; a certificate becomes void when the site changes.

## Reject rule

A product missing a valid OEKO-TEX certificate, or produced by a supplier without a passed audit, must be rejected for launch. The required action is to upload a valid certificate and schedule a supplier audit before market launch.
