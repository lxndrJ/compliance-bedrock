# GPSR — General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023 (Summary)

**Scope:** The GPSR applies to consumer products placed on the EU market, including textiles, sportswear, and hardgoods. It replaces the General Product Safety Directive and applies from 13 December 2024.

**Key obligations for product launches in EU markets:**

1. **Economic operator responsibilities:** Every product must have a responsible economic operator established in the EU whose contact details appear on the product or packaging.
2. **Product safety requirements:** Products must be safe under normal or reasonably foreseeable use. Manufacturers must perform risk assessments before placing products on the market.
3. **Traceability:** Products must be traceable; technical documentation must be kept for 10 years after the product is placed on the market.
4. **Labelling:** Warnings and safety information must be in the language(s) of the member state of sale.
5. **Accident reporting:** Businesses must report product accidents to the EU Safety Gateway (Safety Business Portal) within 24 hours of becoming aware.

**Evidence required for compliance sign-off:**
- A GPSR conformity assessment or declaration covering the product
- A documented risk assessment for the product category
- A designated EU responsible economic operator

**Reject rule:** A product without a valid GPSR conformity declaration must not be launched in any EU market. Missing documentation requires human review by a compliance officer before any launch decision.
